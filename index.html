HUNTING &
OUTDOOR

Most of my recent editing work has been for hunting and outdoor businesses in Australia.

The footage is usually supplied by the client. I handle the edit, story structure, sound, colour and, when needed, remote direction.

PROJECT 01

Living the Hunter Lifestyle

Forest to Fork

A landing film built almost entirely from an existing archive.

Years of footage. Different cameras. Different resolutions. No obvious narrative.

I went through the archive, found the story, then directed a new piece of performance from Luke to give the film a beginning.

The goal wasn't to make an outfitter montage. It was to let someone who had never met Luke understand what spending a few days with him might actually feel like.

PROJECT 02

Bow Hunting in State Forest

Diary of a Hunting Guide / E04

No deer. No conventional payoff.

This episode was about making an anticlimactic hunting trip enjoyable anyway.

Bow hunting naturally creates more tension and uncertainty than a long-range rifle hunt. I leaned into that rather than trying to manufacture a bigger ending.

PROJECT 03

2 Fallow Deer in 1 Arvo

Diary of a Hunting Guide / E03

A successful public-land hunt shaped almost entirely around what the camera managed to capture.

The missing pieces were reconstructed through a recap recorded by the guide.

The edit had to turn disconnected moments into something that felt like one continuous afternoon.

These are older films, but they're important to me. I shot, edited and graded every shot.

They taught me how to look for light, movement, rhythm and atmosphere before I ever started doing this professionally. Please note these shots were in my phase where 120fps was a thing(I've Changed😉)

PROJECT 01

Spiti Valley Through My Eyes

Personal Film

A solo trip through the Himalayas, shot on a Sony A6300.

I documented the entire journey as a personal memoir — the landscapes, the people and the experiences along the way.

It became one of those trips that stays with you long after you've returned home.

Short Cut Version 02
PROJECT 02

Kashmir: Heaven on Earth

Personal Film

A travel film built around contrast.

Srinagar's noise and movement on one side. Dal Lake, the tulip gardens and quieter moments on the other.

More than the locations, I wanted the film to retain the warmth of being welcomed into a place as a stranger.

PROJECT 03

The Kurunji Blooms

Western Ghats / 2018

The Neelakurunji blooms once every twelve years.

I went to the Nilgiris with a camera, a gimbal and very little money, sat down for one long stretch and built this film around the landscape.

An early film, made entirely in Premiere and Lumetri. It later found a surprisingly large audience on Instagram.

PROJECT 04

Chasing Delhi

Personal Film

No elaborate concept.

I found a track I loved, went back through footage from a solo trip and cut the city around its rhythm.

I’m interested in edits that let a story breathe. Not edits that exist purely to prove that an editor was there.
