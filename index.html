I studied computer science. I didn't go to film school.

I learned by making things, breaking things, watching people better than me, and doing the job again.

Since then I've worked with brands, startups, agencies and social-impact organisations across different industries and different parts of the world.

I'm happiest somewhere between the strategy conversation and the timeline.

HOW
I WORK.

01
STORY FIRST.

The edit shouldn't be trying to prove how clever the editor is. The audience should forget there's an edit happening.

02
CUT THE NOISE.

More footage doesn't mean more story. Sometimes the best thing you can do is remove things.

03
DETAILS MATTER.

Sound. Colour. A half-second. The frame before someone speaks. That's where a lot of the feeling lives.

04
MAKE IT USEFUL.

Beautiful is good. But if the video doesn't solve the actual problem, we've made expensive wallpaper.
